Understanding Autism

Autism Spectrum Condition (ASC) is a neurodevelopmental difference that affects how a person communicates, interacts with others, and experiences the world. Every autistic person is unique.

✨ Signs you might notice in children

  • • Delayed or unusual speech development
  • • Avoiding eye contact
  • • Intense focus on specific interests
  • • Difficulty with changes in routine
  • • Sensitivity to sounds, lights, or textures

🌿 Signs in adults (including masking)

  • • Social exhaustion after work/events
  • • Replaying conversations (rumination)
  • • Difficulty with unwritten social rules
  • • Burnout cycles
  • • History of being called "too sensitive"
💡 Remember: Many autistic people, especially women and girls, learn to "mask" their traits. If you see yourself or your child in these descriptions, an assessment can provide clarity.

Understanding ADHD

Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) affects executive functions: working memory, impulse control, organisation, and emotional regulation.

📋 Inattention signs

  • • Losing things constantly
  • • Difficulty finishing tasks
  • • Easily distracted
  • • Poor time management
  • • Daydreaming

⚡ Hyperactivity/Impulsivity signs

  • • Restlessness (internal or physical)
  • • Interrupting conversations
  • • Acting without thinking
  • • Emotional intensity
  • • Difficulty waiting

ADHD often co-occurs with Autism, and anxiety. Our assessment explores all possibilities.

Preparing for Assessment

  • • Gather any school reports, previous assessments, or medical records
  • • Think about early development (milestones, speech, play)
  • • Make notes about specific examples of behaviours or challenges
  • • Complete any questionnaires we send you before the appointment
  • • For remote assessments: test your internet connection and camera beforehand
  • • Prepare questions you want to ask the clinician

What Happens After Diagnosis?

Receiving a diagnosis can bring relief, clarity, and a range of emotions. We support you through:

  • • Comprehensive diagnostic report - accepted by schools, GPs, and employers
  • • Recommendations - for school, work, or home adjustments
  • • Education support - guidance for EHCP applications and SEN support
  • • Post-diagnostic resources - books, websites, and local support groups
  • • Medication pathway - for ADHD where clinically appropriate

Frequently Asked Questions for Families

How long does an assessment take?
Typically 4-6 weeks from referral to receiving your diagnostic report.
Will my child's school accept the diagnosis?
Yes. Our reports are written by HCPC registered clinicians and are recognised by schools and local authorities.
Can adults be assessed without parents involved?
Yes. For adult assessments, we can use alternative sources of developmental history.
Is online assessment as good as in-person?
Yes. ADOS-2 remote administration is clinically validated.
