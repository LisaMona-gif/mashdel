Who we are · What we stand for

Our Vision, Mission & Values

A beacon of hope, understanding, and support

Our Vision

To be a trusted beacon of hope, guidance, and excellence in healthcare, helping individuals and families access high-quality neurodevelopmental assessment and support services.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, compassionate, and evidence-based neurodevelopmental services that empower individuals and families through understanding, support, and expert care.

Compassion

We listen, we care, and we respond with kindness at every step of the journey. Every person who contacts us is treated with dignity and respect.

Excellence

We deliver evidence-based, NICE-aligned assessments of the highest clinical standard. Our team is committed to continuous improvement and professional development.

Integrity

We are honest, transparent, and accountable to our service users, our regulators, and ourselves. We uphold the duty of candour.

Accessibility

We remove barriers to assessment through remote options, reasonable adjustments, and plain language. Healthcare should be available to everyone.

Collaboration

We work with families, schools, GPs, and local authorities for better outcomes. No one should navigate this journey alone.

Hope

We believe that understanding a diagnosis opens doors to support, self-acceptance, and a brighter future. There is always hope.

"A diagnosis is not a label — it's a key that unlocks understanding, support, and a path forward."

— Clinical Director, Mashdel Neurodevelopmental Assessments
