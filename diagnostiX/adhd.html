What is ADHD?

ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder) is a neurodevelopmental condition that affects executive functions — the brain's ability to plan, focus, organise, and regulate emotions. It is not a behavioural problem or a lack of willpower.

📋 Inattention signs:
  • • Losing things constantly
  • • Difficulty finishing tasks
  • • Easily distracted
  • • Poor time management
  • • Daydreaming
⚡ Hyperactivity/Impulsivity signs:
  • • Restlessness (internal or physical)
  • • Interrupting conversations
  • • Acting without thinking
  • • Emotional intensity
  • • Difficulty waiting

For adults with a confirmed ADHD diagnosis, we offer a medication pathway including:

  • Monitoring — regular reviews to ensure effectiveness and safety

Note: Medication is only offered where clinically appropriate and after a full diagnostic assessment.

Frequently asked questions about ADHD

Can I be assessed for ADHD if I'm an adult?
Yes. ADHD is a lifelong condition that often continues into adulthood. We assess adults using the DIVA-5, which is specifically validated for adult ADHD.
Do I need a referral from a GP?
No. You can self-refer directly to us. We accept referrals from individuals, parents, GPs, schools, and other professionals.
Is online assessment as good as in-person?
Yes. DIVA-5 and clinical interviews are validated for remote administration. Our online assessments have the same clinical rigour as in-person appointments.
How much does an ADHD assessment cost?

Can I have a medication review after diagnosis?
Yes. We offer a medication pathway for adults with a confirmed ADHD diagnosis. This includes initial prescription, monitoring, and shared care with your GP.
