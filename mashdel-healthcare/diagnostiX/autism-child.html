What is Autism in children?

Autism Spectrum Condition (ASC) is a neurodevelopmental difference that affects how children communicate, interact with others, and experience the world. Every autistic child is unique, but common themes include differences in social communication, sensory processing, and repetitive behaviours or intense interests.

🔍 Early signs parents notice:
  • • Delayed or unusual speech development
  • • Avoiding eye contact
  • • Intense focus on specific topics or objects
  • • Difficulty with changes in routine (meltdowns)
  • • Playing differently (lining up toys, not pretend play)
🧠 How autism presents in school:
  • • Struggles with group work or friendships
  • • Sensory overload in lunch halls/assemblies
  • • Literal understanding of instructions
  • • Exceptional memory or skill in specific areas

Our child autism assessment pathway

We follow NICE guideline CG142. All assessments are multidisciplinary and family-centred.

1Initial triage call
2Developmental history (ADI-R) with parents
3ADOS-2 assessment (online or clinic)
4School liaison (with consent)
5MDT review & feedback

⏱️ Timeline: 4–6 weeks from referral to diagnostic report.
